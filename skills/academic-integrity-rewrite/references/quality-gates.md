# Quality gates

## P0: integrity and technical fidelity

- Every borrowed idea is attributed; direct wording is quoted when required.
- Numbers, signs, ranges, units, equations, chemical formulae, and statistical qualifiers match the evidence.
- Citation markers remain attached to the claims they support.
- Figure, table, equation, and structure labels agree across text, captions, and conclusions.
- Reference metadata is verified against a DOI record, publisher page, or trusted index before correction.

## P1: reasoning and support

- The paragraph has one identifiable purpose and a defensible evidence chain.
- The interpretation is supported by the stated experiment, simulation, or model resolution.
- Correlation is not rewritten as causation.
- Limitations and uncertainty constrain the relevant claim.
- Literature is synthesized rather than listed source by source.

## P2: academic expression

- Subjects and comparison baselines are explicit.
- Verbs are precise; vague announcements and self-evident claims are removed.
- Terminology, tense, abbreviations, hyphenation, and singular/plural forms are consistent.
- Technical terms are not replaced merely to appear different.

## P3: presentation and submission readiness

- Units, symbols, spacing, subscripts, captions, and cross-references follow the target guide.
- Headings, captions, references, and equations use stable styles or automatic numbering where available.
- The final editable file and exported PDF are checked page by page for missing labels, clipping, and broken pagination.

## Release decision

Block delivery on any unresolved P0 issue. Deliver with explicit verification notes for unresolved P1 items. P2 and P3 findings may be prioritized according to the user's requested scope.
